In memory of Julio Paez

The man behind the collection

Julio collected remarkable ideas by hand. He cut the pages that caught his attention, organized them and made his own books. This is a tribute to his curiosity, craftsmanship and the things he wanted to preserve.

One project at a time. For Grandpa Julio.
