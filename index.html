The dedication page

For Grandpa Julio

Julio Paez collected remarkable ideas the old-fashioned way: by cutting pages out of magazines, organizing them, and turning them into books of his own. Woodworking, homemade machines, clever furniture—projects worth keeping.

Those handmade books were lost. This collection is a tribute to his curiosity and the care he put into saving the ideas that interested him.

May these pages feel a little like opening one of his books again.
